Methodology & provenance

What this is

SFERA Lab is a research instrument, not a commercial product. This page computes real Western tropical natal charts and displays them with their conventions labeled. It generates no AI text. It exists because the SFERA research program needs a verified deterministic calculation layer, and because a free, honest chart tool is genuinely useful. This is a Research UI, deliberately not a finished product interface.

Calculation method

LayerMethod
Planetary positionsastronomy-engine 2.1.19 (MIT, analytic ephemeris — the same library version as the verified Python kernel), geocentric ecliptic-of-date longitudes; retrograde states from numeric 1-hour central-difference speeds.
Time zonesIANA tzdb via the browser/Node Intl database — the authoritative source for historical offsets, DST and LMT-era births. Nonexistent local times (DST spring gaps) are rejected, not silently shifted. Ambiguous fall-back times are labeled and resolved explicitly (fold 0/1).
HousesNative formulas for ASC/MC and Placidus, equal and whole-sign systems, computed in your browser. House systems genuinely disagree away from the angles — that is a documented feature of the tradition; the system used is always labeled. Koch, Regiomontanus and Campanus are computed by the Python lab kernel but are not implemented in this browser build (honestly absent, never approximated).
Lunar nodesMean node via the analytic secular formula. The true (osculating) node requires the lab kernel's library and is suppressed here.
AspectsMajor aspects (0/60/90/120/180) with one documented natal orb set (config orbs-v0.1); the convention is disclosed rather than hidden.
Suppression ruleUnknown birth time → Moon degree, angles and houses are suppressed, never approximated. Beyond ±66° latitude angles and houses are suppressed (school-dependent to the point of being undefined).

School & convention labels

Western tropical astrology is not one thing. Where a convention matters (house system, orb set, node type), this tool shows which one was used. Statements about meaning come only from the labeled static template, which describes the calculated chart and deliberately suppresses what is unknown.

Research status (honest)

The lab has run an internal quality benchmark of AI interpretation (X-01-Z) and maintains an evidence database and claim ledger — see the live research status page, generated from the repository. What is not validated: human practitioner acceptance, the engine bet on voluntary return, willingness to pay. No research claims are made beyond what exists.

Privacy

The calculation runs in your browser; birth data never leaves your device; there is no server to receive it. Full explanation: privacy page.

City list disclosure

The city search uses a static bundled list of 161 cities compiled by the research agent (coordinates ≈±1 km, verified class: UNVERIFIED_COMPILED_LIST). For archival precision, enter exact latitude/longitude and the IANA time zone manually.
