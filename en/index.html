Free natal chart — computed from ephemeris data, not generated by AI

SFERA Lab is an experimental research environment for computational astrology, interpretation systems and AI-assisted comparative research. This page computes a Western (tropical) natal chart with a deterministic engine that is differentially verified against the lab's Python kernel (260 frozen cases, 0 failures — engine agreement, not proof of truth; methodology), with full disclosure of method, conventions and school labels. No AI text is generated here. The calculation runs entirely in your browser — your birth data never leaves your device. No accounts, no tracking, nothing stored. 18+ only. How it works, and what it is not.
Measurement, precisely stated: without your consent nothing is sent — not even the fact of a visit. If you tick the consent box, anonymous usage events (funnel stages, no birth data) may be sent to a minimal research sink. Exactly what is sent: the privacy explanation.
Why exact birth time matters

The Ascendant, MC and house cusps shift roughly every 4 minutes of birth time, so they cannot be determined without it. Planets' positions within signs barely move during a day. If you don't know the time, don't invent one: the tool will honestly hide what cannot be computed without it (Moon degree, angles, houses) and show what can (planets in signs, planetary aspects).

Koch and other quadrant systems are not computed in this browser build (see methodology); the Python lab kernel supports more systems.
