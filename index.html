Free natal chart — calculated, not invented

SFERA Lab is an experimental research environment for computational astrology, interpretation systems and AI-assisted comparative research. This page computes a research-grade Western (tropical) natal chart with a differentially verified deterministic engine, full disclosure of method, conventions and school labels. No AI text is generated here. The calculation runs entirely in your browser — your birth data never leaves your device. No accounts, no cookies, nothing stored. 18+ only. How it works, and what it is not.
Measurement, precisely stated: this research surface has no central telemetry — there is no server to receive anything. If you tick the consent box, anonymous usage measurement is enabled in your browser only; see the privacy explanation for exactly what that means.
Koch and other quadrant systems are not computed in this browser build (see methodology); the Python lab kernel supports more systems.
